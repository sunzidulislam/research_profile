I am an Assistant Programmer at [iAutomationBD](https://www.iautomationbd.com/) in Dhaka, Bangladesh, and a part-time Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at [Southeast University](https://seu.edu.bd/). Previously, I served as a Junior Data Scientist at [ConneqtedAgents](https://conneqtedagents.ai/). I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from [KUET](https://www.kuet.ac.bd/cse) (Khulna University of Engineering & Technology), which I received in 2025. Through my roles as a Research Assistant and Teaching Assistant across various institutions, I have cultivated hands-on expertise in both academic research and software engineering.

In addition to my industry roles, I am currently a Research Collaborator at the [SPEED Lab](https://www.fau.edu/research/speedlab/)  under the supervision of [Ahmed Imtaj](https://ai.fau.edu/people/ahmed-imtaj/). My research is heavily focused on advancing **3D Image Generation** and **Digital Twins**, exploring how cutting-edge generative AI can create highly accurate, interactive virtual models. Alongside these core areas, my interests also encompass Vision-Language Models (VLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Diffusion Transformers, and Federated Learning. I am deeply passionate about bridging the gap between research innovation and scalable engineering to develop robust, next-generation AI systems.

I am actively open to research collaborations with academic groups, laboratories, and industry partners.
